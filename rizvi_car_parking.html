About Us

Safe Parking,
Peace of Mind

Rizvi Car Parking was established in March 2021 to solve a real problem for the residents of Nasarpur — the lack of safe, accessible parking space for those without direct car access at home.

Located on Tando Allahyar Road alongside the Government Water Supply and Government Civil Hospital, Nasarpur, we offer both daily and monthly parking plans in a secure, well-lit facility accessible around the clock.

Whether you own a car and need a safe, dedicated space every day, we have a spot for you — managed with care and professionalism.
